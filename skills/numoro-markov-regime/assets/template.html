Method

Data
[[method_data]]
Chain
Daily returns split into five equal bands at [[edges]]. Transition probabilities counted from [[n_pairs]] consecutive day pairs. Chi-square tests check whether tomorrow's direction, or band, depends on today's band.
Regimes
Gaussian hidden Markov model on daily log returns with three states, ordered by volatility; best of eight random starts. [[bic_sentence]]
Backtest
[[warmup_txt]] warm-up, then [[bt_start]] to [[bt_end]]. Each decision uses data up to that day's close and applies to the next session. Chain rule: hold [[ticker]] when today's band has had a positive average next day so far, otherwise cash. Stress filter: refit every quarter on all data to date, cash when the filtered stress probability is above 50%. 5 basis points per switch. Cash earns nothing, which slightly understates both timing rules when T-bill rates are high. No taxes.

Statistical description of past prices, for information only. Not investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any security.
